### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My QR code won't scan. What's wrong?**
A: Make sure your input is correctly formatted. For URLs, include the full address (e.g. `example.com` — the app auto-adds `https://`). For WiFi, double-check the SSID and password match your network exactly (both are case-sensitive).

**Q: How do I save my QR code to Photos?**
A: Tap the Save button below the QR code. The first time, iOS will ask permission to add to Photos — tap Allow. Your QR will be saved at high resolution.

**Q: Can I remove the ads?**
A: Not currently. The app is free and supported entirely by lightweight ads.

**Q: What's the difference between Cute and Pro themes?**
A: Cute is a playful, pastel design — great for personal use. Pro is sleek and minimal — better for business and professional contexts. Both generate identical, fully scannable QR codes.

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: Yes. QR code generation works completely offline. Only the ad banner requires an internet connection.

# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 20, 2026**

Quick QR Code Generator ("the App") is developed by xudong li. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

## TL;DR

The App generates QR codes on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the content you type. We do not track you. We display non-personalized third-party ads through Google AdMob.

## Information We Collect Directly

**None.** All QR code generation happens entirely on your device. URLs, WiFi passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, and any text you enter to create a QR code are processed locally and never sent to us or any third party.

We do not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any personal identifiers.

## No Tracking

The App does **not** track you. It does not link your activity to data from other apps or websites for advertising purposes. It does not share data with data brokers.

## Information Collected by Third Parties

The App displays advertisements through **Google AdMob**, a service provided by Google LLC. The App is configured to request **non-personalized ads only**. For ad delivery, AdMob may collect:

- Device type, operating system, and version
- Approximate location (based on IP address)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

These are used solely to deliver and measure ads, not to build a personalized advertising profile of you.

Google AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Photos Permission

The App requests permission to save QR code images to your Photos library. This is a one-way "add only" permission — the App cannot read or access your existing photos. Saved images stay on your device and are never uploaded.

## Crash Data

Apple may collect basic crash data on your device to help us improve app stability. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and contains no personal information you have entered.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
